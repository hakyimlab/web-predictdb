We developed a new framework MetaboXcan that links genetically predicted metabolites, gene expression, and complex traits. 
The [preprint](https://doi.org/10.64898/2026.07.15.738631) is available on BioRxiv. 

The metabolite prediction models utilized can be downloaded [here](https://uchicago.box.com/s/acryijtnxltz5a0dpf1qdkljwyxcodjn) and 
gene models from [here](https://predictdb.org/post/2024/11/11/twas-inflation-corrected-models/).

The MetaboXcan software is available from [GitHub](https://github.com/hakyimlab/metaboxcan), please report any issues on GitHub.
